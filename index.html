Black
White

    New game

    Rules

    Goal: line up 5 stones, or capture 5 pairs.

    Capture: put your stones on both sides of exactly 2 enemy stones and they're removed. Playing in between 2 enemy stones yourself is safe.

    Breaking a five: a five only wins if the other player can't capture a pair out of it. If he can, he has to do it right away.

    Double three: a free three is 3 stones that become an unstoppable four if nobody blocks them. You can't make 2 of them with one move, except if that move captures.

    Openings: with Pro, the first stone goes in the middle and black's second stone at least 3 cells away from it (4 with Long pro).
